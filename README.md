# 📡 Kabootar - Chat without internet or servers

[![Download Kabootar](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Kabootar-blue)](https://github.com/Grouperecclescake78/Kabootar)

Kabootar lets you send messages without an internet connection. It works through your phone's Bluetooth and Wi-Fi signals. You do not need a SIM card or a central server to talk to people nearby. The app moves messages from phone to phone until they reach the person you want to contact.

## 🛠 How It Works

Kabootar uses a network that tolerates delays. When you send a message, the app stores it locally. It waits until it finds another device running Kabootar. The message hops between devices like a relay race. This system keeps working even when signals fail or servers go down. 

The software uses end-to-end acknowledgments. This ensures your message arrives at the destination. Your chat logs stay on your device. No third party sees your private data.

## ⚙️ Initial Setup

When you open Kabootar for the first time, the screen shows you a unique identification code. This code represents your device in the mesh network.

1. Pick a display name for your profile. 
2. Grant the app permission to use your Bluetooth and Wi-Fi radios. The app needs these to discovery nearby devices.
3. Stay within range of other users to establish a link.
4. Once you see another device in your list, you can start a chat.

## 💡 Tips for Best Performance

The mesh network relies on people moving around. Here are ways to ensure your messages get delivered:

* Keep your Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on.
* Do not close the app fully while waiting for messages. Keep it running in the background.
* Move to an open area if you cannot find nearby devices. Thick concrete walls can block radio signals.
* Be patient. Since the app uses hop-by-hop delivery, messages take time to travel long distances.

## 🛡 Privacy and Safety

Kabootar does not store your messages on a cloud server. Everything stays on your hardware. Because of this, you keep full control over your chat history. You can clear your history or delete specific threads at any time through the settings menu.

## 🔧 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this app work without any internet?**
Yes. It uses peer-to-peer technology to build a network using your device hardware.

**Can I talk to someone in another city?**
Kabootar works best for people in the same building or neighborhood. It cannot bridge global distances without many users acting as relay points in between.

**Is it safe?**
The app employs encryption to protect your conversations. Only the person you intend to message can read the content.

**Why does the app ask for location access?**
Operating systems require location permissions to manage Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning hardware. The app uses this access only to find other nearby devices.
