It is **not** affiliated with, authorised by, or endorsed by the Government of

## Use of national symbols

- The **national tricolour** (saffron, white, green) and the **Ashoka Chakra**
  are used respectfully, as a mark of national pride, consistent with the spirit
  of the Flag Code of India, 2002.
- We deliberately **do not** use the **State Emblem of India** (the Lion Capital
  of Ashoka), whose use is reserved for government authorities under the State
  Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005.
- The **Preamble** and the **Fundamental Duties (Article 51A)** shown in the app
  are reproduced from the Constitution of India for civic and educational
  purposes, and are public record.

If you believe any use here is inappropriate or incorrect, please open an issue
and it will be addressed promptly.
